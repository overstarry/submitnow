# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it when you use submitnow.

## What submitnow does

submitnow is a web workbench and open-source toolkit that helps you verify key files, import sitemaps, and send IndexNow submissions to `api.indexnow.org` or participating search engine endpoints (Bing, Yandex, Seznam.cz, Naver, and Yep). We do not provide user accounts.

## Information you provide

### IndexNow submission, key check, and remote sitemap inputs

When you use our submission form or server-assisted utilities, you may provide:
- Your IndexNow key and optional `host` / `keyLocation` overrides
- A URL list (automatically chunked into batches of up to 10,000 URLs) and selected IndexNow endpoint
- A public sitemap URL or domain when using the optional remote sitemap fetcher

We process this information in memory on our server solely to run your requested verification, fetch public XML sitemaps, or forward your IndexNow payload to the chosen endpoint. We do not store submitted keys, URL lists, or fetched sitemaps in an application database.

### Browser-local utilities and optional device storage

Tools such as the Key Generator, URL Normalizer, Batch Builder, local XML Sitemap Diff, and Code Generator run entirely in your browser without sending your inputs to our server.

If you opt in to saving site profiles, sitemap baselines, or keeping local submission history, that data (including saved host/key profiles and up to 200 URLs per history entry for quick reload) is stored exclusively in your browser's `localStorage` on your device and can be cleared at any time from the UI.

### Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, you provide your email address. We use it to send emails and to manage your subscription (including cryptographically signed unsubscribe links).

## Information we collect automatically

When you use the site, we may collect:
- Basic request metadata (such as IP address and User-Agent) for security, SSRF/abuse prevention, rate limiting, and debugging
- Coarse analytics data about how the site is used (such as page views and interaction counts; submitted keys, hosts, and URLs are never sent to analytics)
- Preference data stored on your device (such as language and theme settings)

## Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and/or local storage for preference features (for example, language selection, dismissing the language banner, theme choice, and optional local profiles/history). Depending on deployment configuration, the site may also load analytics or advertising scripts in production.

## How we share information

We share information only as needed to provide the service:
- IndexNow endpoints (`api.indexnow.org`, Bing/Microsoft, Yandex, Seznam.cz, Naver, or Yep): IndexNow payloads are forwarded to the endpoint you select
- Target public servers: when you run a key check or remote sitemap fetch, our server requests the public URL you specified
- Email provider: newsletter delivery and subscriber management is handled by our email provider
- Analytics and advertising providers: production deployments may load enabled analytics or ad scripts
- Legal and safety: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the service from abuse

## Data retention

- IndexNow submission, key-check, and remote sitemap inputs: processed transiently in memory and not stored in an application database
- Newsletter email: retained until you unsubscribe (or we delete it)
- Rate-limiting records: IP-based counters may be retained temporarily for abuse prevention
- Browser-local storage: remains on your device until you clear it in the UI or browser settings

## Your choices

You can:
- Unsubscribe from the newsletter using the signed unsubscribe link in any email
- Clear saved site profiles, sitemap baselines, or local submission history directly from the interface
- Control cookies and local storage via your browser settings

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact us via the email address listed in the site footer or via the source code repository.
