# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it when you use submitnow.

## What submitnow does

submitnow is a web UI that helps you send IndexNow submissions to the Bing IndexNow endpoint. We do not provide user accounts.

## Information you provide

### IndexNow submission data

When you submit a request, you may provide:
- Your IndexNow key
- A URL list (up to 10,000 URLs per request)
- Optional `host` and `keyLocation` overrides

We process this information to build your request and send it to Bing. We do not store your IndexNow submission inputs in an application database.

### Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, you provide your email address. We use it to send emails and to manage your subscription (including unsubscribe).

## Information we collect automatically

When you use the site, we may collect:
- Basic request metadata (such as IP address and user agent) for security, abuse prevention, and debugging
- Analytics data about how the site is used (for example, page views and general device/browser information)
- Preference data stored on your device (such as language and theme settings)

## Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and/or local storage for preference features (for example, remembering your language choice, remembering that you dismissed the language suggestion, and theme selection). We may also use cookies for analytics and advertising depending on which integrations are enabled on the deployment.

## How we share information

We share information only as needed to provide the service:
- Bing / Microsoft: IndexNow submissions are sent to the Bing IndexNow endpoint
- Email provider: newsletter delivery and subscriber management is handled by our email provider
- Analytics and advertising providers: the site may load analytics and ads scripts in production
- Legal and safety: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the service from abuse

## Data retention

- IndexNow submission inputs: processed to send your request and not stored in an application database
- Newsletter email: retained until you unsubscribe (or we delete it)
- Abuse-prevention data: if rate limiting is enabled, rate-limit records may be retained for a limited period
- Analytics: retained according to the provider settings

## Your choices

You can:
- Unsubscribe from the newsletter using the unsubscribe link in any email
- Control cookies and local storage via your browser settings (some preference features may not work if disabled)

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact us via the email address listed in the site footer or via the source code repository.

## Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Please review it periodically.
